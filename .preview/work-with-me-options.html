How I'd choose

  • A if you want the homepage to stay exactly as light as it is now.
  • B only if you expect most freelance clients to arrive on the homepage and never click anything.
  • C if you want the homepage to show you have a process (that alone reassures clients) while the long detail stays on its own page.
