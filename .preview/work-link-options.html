Today the only way in is: homepage, then "Work with me", then a small underlined link. Four ways to make it easier to find. They can be combined: my pick is 2 together with 3.

A plain link in the top bar

One more item, "Work with me", next to Blog and Art & Leisure. It's visible on every page, and also in the slim bar that returns while scrolling. It looks like the other items, so it's easy to overlook.
